08 · Critical rules

What must never
break.

These rules keep the system reliable as more staff, developers and agents work on it.

01

No invented facts.

Unknown hours, prices, capacity, rules or services remain unknown until verified.

02

Request ≠ booking.

A reservation ticket becomes confirmed only after staff validation.

03

Brands stay distinct.

Grupo Pipa connects businesses without flattening their identities.

04

Media has provenance.

Assets carry source, verification, publication and rights state.

05

Mature tools first.

Use WhatsApp, Booking and Maps before rebuilding mature infrastructure without proven need.

06

AI comes later.

Automation and AI sit on top of reliable data and real operations.
